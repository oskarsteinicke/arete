# Arete — character progression

Twelve images: six stages of one character, in a male and a female version.
They are the player character in a premium fitness and self-improvement app.
Stage one is someone just starting. Stage six is someone who arrived.

## The look

Modern, restrained character art. Beautiful and atmospheric, not flat and not
sterile. Think *Sky: Children of the Light*, *Gris*, *Journey* — clean confident
work with a limited palette, where the mood does the talking.

The character should feel like quiet strength. Someone who trains every day and
has nothing to prove. Not a hero, not a warrior, not a god.

Warm stone, ivory and gold against deep black. Beyond that, make it good.

## The six stages

Write each as its own image. Let the figure grow into themselves.

**1.** A young athlete standing in darkness, calm and unadorned. Simple clothing,
nothing that marks them out yet. They look like they have just begun.

**2.** The same person, more settled. They stand differently — shoulders back,
weight grounded. A first thread of gold somewhere on them.

**3.** Stronger now, visibly. The body of someone who has been consistent for a
long time. Gold catches in more than one place.

**4.** Something falls from one shoulder now, changing their shape in the dark.
They have presence they did not have before.

**5.** A long garment reaching near the floor, the fabric catching light. They
look composed and unhurried. Gold runs along the edges.

**6.** Fully themselves. Luminous, still, standing in their own light. The most
striking of the six. Someone who has become what they were working toward.

## The only real rules

- **Full body, standing, facing forward, on a solid black background.** Same
  framing every time so they sit identically side by side.
- **They must be the same person across all six.** Consistency matters more than
  any single image being perfect.
- **No togas, no laurel crowns, no sandals, no Greek key patterns, no armour.**
  That is what the old set looked like and it is the thing being replaced.
- **512 x 768 PNG.** Under 150 KB each.

Everything else is yours.

## Male and female

Make the male set first, then the female set from it. Same clothing, same
palette, same stages, same mood — a different person, equally strong. Do not
make the female version prettier, softer, or more decorated. She trains too.
