How it works

It's not motivation. It's data.

Wanting to change is easy. Seeing what you actually did is what moves you. Arete keeps your habits, food, training and reflections in one place, so the pattern is in front of you every day.

  • Habits

    Tick off your daily habits and see how often you showed up this week, not just a streak.

  • Nutrition

    Log a meal by typing it, snapping a photo or repeating yesterday's in one tap.

  • Training

    Follow a program or describe a goal and get one built. The weight goes up when you have earned it.

  • Journal

    Three questions each evening: what went well, what you learned, what comes next.

  • AI Coach

    Ask it anything. It reads your training, food and sleep and answers in plain words.
