The difference

One connected system.

Your habit app doesn't know you trained legs. Your calorie app doesn't know you slept 5 hours. Arete's modules talk to each other.

01

Training drives nutrition

Log a heavy session and today's carbs and protein rise automatically. Rest day? Carbs come down. Your targets follow your training — not a static number.

02

A daily readiness score

Every morning: one number built from your sleep, training load, habits and nutrition. Green means push. Red means recover. No $300 wearable required.

03

A coach that already knows

The AI coach reads your week — training, food, sleep, journal — and calls you out unprompted when things drift. A coach, not a chatbot.

The story

Built by someone who actually uses it.

"I got tired of juggling five different apps for habits, workouts and food. So I built the one I actually wanted to use."

Arete is a solo project — built by a D1 college athlete between training sessions and classes. No venture capital, no growth hacks, no dark patterns.

Your data lives on your device. No account required, no tracking beyond basic analytics. You own your progress — export it, delete it, whatever you want.

This is early. Things might break. But improvements ship every week, and early users get a direct line to the developer.
